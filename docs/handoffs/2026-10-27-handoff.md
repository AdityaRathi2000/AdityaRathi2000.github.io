## 7. Traps that cost time

- **CI hang:** `playwright install --with-deps` ran apt and stalled 40 min on an Ubuntu mirror. Fixed at
  `.github/workflows/ci.yml:105` (no `--with-deps`) + `timeout-minutes` on every job. Never re-add `--with-deps`.
- **Node 24 (CI) vs Node 26 (local):** `node --test tests/unit/` works on 26, fails on 24 ("Cannot find module").
  Use the glob at `package.json:21`. Reproduce CI-only failures with `npx -y node@24 ...`.
- **Astro 7 `astro preview` children outlive the pnpm wrapper.** Spawn detached and kill the process group;
  `pkill -f "astro preview ..."` kills your own shell (pattern is in its command line). A stray server on :4330
  was found and stopped during this handoff.
- **Astro drops the space** between a component and text on the next line (`<Metric .../>\n  US` → "165MUS").
  Keep the following word on the same line (`src/components/tiles/Tile.astro:52`).
- **YAML types:** `as_of: 2026` is a number and `2026-10-26` a Date → normalised at `src/content.config.ts:15`;
  `period: 2026` coerced at `src/content.config.ts:76`. Apply the same to any new date-ish field.
- **Literal-number check misses magnitudes** unless the K/M/B suffix is handled — fixed at
  `scripts/check-no-literal-numbers.mjs:30`; fixtures in `tests/fixtures/numbers/` must keep failing on `600M`.
- **Lighthouse locally needs `--collect.settings.chromeFlags="--no-sandbox --headless=new"`** and
  `CHROME_PATH=~/.cache/ms-playwright/chromium-1248/chrome-linux64/chrome` (Ubuntu AppArmor blocks the sandbox).
  Never put `--no-sandbox` in `lighthouserc.json`; CI does not need it.
- **LCP headroom is thin:** text pages 1.35 s vs 1.5 s budget (mobile sim). `/tiles/` (italic font) 1.65 s, so it is
  excluded from LHCI (`lighthouserc.json:5`). Decide the italic-font strategy before the P4 hero ships.
- **Satori needs WOFF/TTF, not WOFF2** — OG fonts come from `@fontsource/piazzolla` (`src/lib/og.ts:19`).
