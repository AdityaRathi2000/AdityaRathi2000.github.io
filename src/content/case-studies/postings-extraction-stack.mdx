## Why it mattered

Job postings are long and inconsistent, and what a job asks for (education, experience, skills, certifications) is buried inside them. Downstream classifiers need those sections cut out first.

## Approach

**Pretraining.** Domain-adaptive pretraining across <Metric id="dapt-encoders" /> encoder architectures, run by an agent-driven experiment loop with automated hyperparameter search. Models were compared on bits-per-byte, which does not depend on the tokenizer. The selected ModernBERT checkpoint is the backbone of the stack.

**Serving.** FP8 quantisation, FlashAttention-2 and minimal-padding batching on RTX 5090s.

**Extraction.** A tiered cascade. A fine-tuned ModernBERT NER model segments each posting into <Metric id="ner-section-types" /> section types (education, experience, skills, certifications, responsibilities and more), which feed several downstream classifiers. Its labels were distilled from an LLM over a weak-supervision-stratified sample and audited by interns; it reaches <Metric id="ner-f1" /> against the gold set.
