## Why it mattered

The labels feed the Burning Glass Institute's internal research.

## Problem

<Metric id="edu-records" /> unlabelled education records, spread across university, K-12 and international taxonomies.

## Constraints

There were no labels at the start, the cost is counted per million records, and the records keep changing over time.

## Approach

Domain-specialised models with selective prediction: each model answers only when it is confident enough and holds the rest back. Task-adaptive fine-tuning replaced a frozen encoder. The models ship through CI/CD, and drift in the incoming records triggers retraining.

## Result

On the <Metric id="edu-us-slice" /> US slice the models reach <Metric id="edu-accuracy" /> accuracy at <Metric id="edu-coverage" /> coverage. Task-adaptive fine-tuning added <Metric id="edu-taft-f1-lift" /> F1 over the frozen encoder.
