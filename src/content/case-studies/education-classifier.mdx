## Approach

Domain-specialised models, one per taxonomy, with selective prediction: each model answers only when it is confident enough and holds the rest back. Task-adaptive fine-tuning replaced a frozen encoder. The models ship through CI/CD, and drift in the incoming records triggers retraining.

## Result

On the <Metric id="edu-us-slice" /> US slice the models reach <Metric id="edu-accuracy" /> accuracy at <Metric id="edu-coverage" /> coverage. Task-adaptive fine-tuning added <Metric id="edu-taft-f1-lift" /> F1 over the frozen encoder.
