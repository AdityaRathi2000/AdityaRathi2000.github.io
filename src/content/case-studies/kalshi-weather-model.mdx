This page reports how good the forecasts are and how the model is tested. It does not report profit, positions or trade sizes.

## Why it mattered

Kalshi lists CFTC-regulated contracts on each day's high temperature in <Metric id="kalshi-cities" /> US cities. A forecast is only useful there if its probabilities are calibrated: a bracket priced at thirty per cent should happen about that often.

## Approach

A model trained on CRPS blends several national numerical weather prediction models and AI weather models into calibrated probabilities for each temperature bracket. Positions are sized with fractional Kelly and capped per cluster of correlated cities.

Every model change is decided by a pre-registered, leakage-safe walk-forward backtest with block-bootstrap confidence intervals. A statistical gate decides when a shadow model is promoted to live, and execution fails closed: it reconciles every order with the exchange and stops on any mismatch.
