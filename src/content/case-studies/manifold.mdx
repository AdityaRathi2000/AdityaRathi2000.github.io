## Why it mattered

A property developer in Dubai needed construction-site photos sorted by trade and phase (structural, MEP, finishing, sitework), and paid for an API that does it.

## Problem

Before the API, the client sorted site photos by hand.

<ProductVideo />

## Approach

The construction taxonomy is encoded into the prompting of Qwen3.5-9B, served on AWS SageMaker. The API has key-based authentication, usage-based pricing and published documentation.
