## Why it mattered

The [Credential Value Index](https://credentialvalueindex.org) is one of the most comprehensive public indices of non-degree credentials. States use it to judge programmes; Idaho has used it to assess programmes for LAUNCH grant funding. Every credential has to be tied to the provider that offers it, and providers arrive from each source under their own names.

## Problem

The same provider appears across <Metric id="cvi-sources" /> sources with different names and details. Comparing every record with every other does not scale, and no single similarity score separates a near-duplicate from a different organisation with a similar name.

## Approach

Four stages, each narrowing the work for the next:

1. **Blocking** keeps comparisons among records that share a key.
2. **Embedding similarity** with FAISS proposes candidate pairs inside each block.
3. **LLM adjudication** decides the candidate pairs.
4. **Graph clustering** turns accepted pairs into canonical providers.

A separate LLM cascade writes a description for each provider; the hardest residuals go to a final tier that uses grounded search.

## Result

Canonical providers grew <Metric id="cvi-canonical-growth" /> year over year. The cascade generated <Metric id="cvi-descriptions" /> provider descriptions and recovered <Metric id="cvi-residual-recovery" /> of the hardest residuals with grounded search.
